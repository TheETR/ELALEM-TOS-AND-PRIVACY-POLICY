**Last updated:** 23 August 2026

ELALEM is a Discord bot offered free of charge as a hobby project. These terms
cover the basic rules for using it, what you can expect from the service, and
where its limits are.

They are intentionally readable. Making this document fifty pages long would
not make the bot more trustworthy, it would just make sure nobody reads it.

Using ELALEM, talking to EMSALI, running a command, or adding the bot to a server
means you accept these terms.

If you add ELALEM to a server, you also confirm that you have permission to add
and configure bots there.

If you do not agree with these terms, stop using ELALEM and remove it from any
server you control.

## 3. What ELALEM does

ELALEM currently includes features such as:

- **Music** with playback, queues, playlists and AutoDJ;
- **EMSALI**, an AI assistant with optional long-term memory;
- **Economy** with virtual currency, rewards and inventory;
- **Fishing** with progression, catches, equipment and locations;
- **Marketplace** for trading virtual cosmetic items with virtual currency;
- **Cosmetics and profiles** with levels, achievements and collectables;
- **Community Moments**, a summary of a server's week;
- **Moderation and Sentinel** anti-raid tools;
- **Game news** and public free-game listings;
- **Server configuration** and feature controls.

ELALEM changes over time. Features may be added, changed, limited or removed.
Nothing in this list is a promise that a feature will exist forever.

## 4. Use ELALEM reasonably

Do not:

- exploit bugs, duplication issues, race conditions or similar problems for an
  unfair advantage in Economy, Fishing, Marketplace or other progression
  systems;
- automate ELALEM with self-bots, scripts or macros in a way that abuses the
  service or creates unreasonable load;
- use ELALEM to harass, threaten, abuse or spam people;
- use ELALEM to create or distribute content that breaks Discord's rules or the
  law;
- try to obtain private system instructions, credentials or restricted service
  information;
- try to access another person's private ELALEM data;
- sell, resell or exchange ELALEM virtual currency or virtual items for real
  money or anything else with real-world value;
- bypass a restriction, suspension or moderation action.

If you find a serious exploit, reporting it is much better than turning it into
a personal economy expansion pack.

## 5. Virtual items have no real-world value

ELALEM balances, inventory items, cosmetics, fishing catches and marketplace
items are virtual in-service records.

They are not real money, securities, investments or a store of value. They
cannot be redeemed for real money or exchanged for anything with real-world
value.

The Marketplace is a game feature for moving virtual cosmetic items between
players using ELALEM's virtual currency. It is not a financial service, exchange,
trading platform or investment product.

**Casino-style games.** Features such as blackjack, roulette, slots, dice,
coinflip and crash use only ELALEM's virtual balance. There is no real-money
purchase, deposit, cash-out or conversion to or from outside value.

Virtual balances and items may sometimes be adjusted, reset or removed to fix an
exploit, correct a bug or support a feature change. Because they have no
real-world value, ELALEM does not offer cash compensation for those changes.

## 6. EMSALI and AI answers

EMSALI uses language models to generate answers. Those answers can be wrong,
outdated, incomplete or overly confident.

Do not treat EMSALI as a substitute for a doctor, lawyer, financial adviser,
emergency service or another qualified professional when a decision could have
serious consequences.

EMSALI's generated answers are not personal statements or endorsements from the
operator of ELALEM.

Some sensitive categories of information cannot be saved as long-term EMSALI
memory, even if you ask. The [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) explains those
rules and how EMSALI data is handled.

## 7. Moderation and Sentinel

Servers can choose to use ELALEM's moderation and Sentinel features. These tools
may act automatically on patterns that look like spam, raids or other abuse.

Automated moderation is not perfect. It can make mistakes and it can miss real
problems. ELALEM's tools are there to help server moderators, not replace them.
Server administrators and moderators remain responsible for how they configure
and use moderation in their own servers.

ELALEM's operator does not personally moderate other people's servers.

## 8. Availability

ELALEM is provided free of charge, as-is and as-available. There is no uptime
guarantee.

The bot can be offline, slow, rate-limited or partly unavailable. It also relies
on Discord and other services for things such as music, metadata and AI features.
If one of those services changes, reaches a limit or becomes unavailable, the
ELALEM feature that depends on it may stop working too.

For AI features, ELALEM may refuse a request temporarily instead of sending the
same user content through a provider that does not meet ELALEM's privacy rules.

Features may be changed, restricted or discontinued without advance notice.

## 11. Privacy and deletion

The [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) explains what ELALEM stores, what may be
sent to other services, how long data is kept and what you can remove.

**`/deletion`** is the main self-service data control inside Discord. It can show,
export and delete eligible data connected to your Discord account without asking
you to enter your user ID or email address again.

Some shared or integrity-related records may need to remain, including parts of
completed transactions and a server's own moderation/security history. ELALEM
shows retained categories instead of pretending they were deleted.

When a server removes ELALEM, that server's own data is scheduled for deletion
after 30 days. Re-adding ELALEM during that window cancels the pending deletion.
A member's own global Economy, Fishing, Cosmetics, Profile and EMSALI data is not
deleted just because one server removes the bot.

For privacy questions, retained records or anything an automated deletion could
not finish, start with the
[ELALEM support server](https://discord.gg/JDKjRhpq79). If you cannot use the
support server, you can also write to **privacy@elalem.dev**.
